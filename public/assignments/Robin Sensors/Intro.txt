This starter code won't make any sound by itself, but it will print note on and off messages to the console and graph the accelerometer data.
- normally you won't graph all of the CC data like this, but it can be helpful to understand the sensor data

* pads: buttons 0 to 10
	* buttons 0 to 7 are the note pads, 8 and 9 are the arrows, 10 is the long strip
* Graphs: 
	* Orange:pitch, roll
	* Blue: acceleration magnitude
* potentiometer just prints to the console
